1. What Are Cookies

A cookie is a small text file that a website places on your device (computer, tablet, mobile phone) when you visit the site. Cookies are used to remember your preferences, recognize you when you return, understand how you use the site, and improve your experience.

Similar technologies include: local storage (HTML5), session storage, web beacons, pixel tags, and JavaScript-based tracking. This policy covers all such technologies collectively referred to as "cookies."

2. Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential operation: cookies required for the service to function, including authentication, load balancing, and security (e.g., Cloudflare security cookies).
  • Functionality: cookies that remember your preferences, such as language, theme, and login state.
  • Analytics: cookies that help us understand how visitors use our site, what works, and what doesn't, so we can improve the service.
  • Performance: cookies that measure site speed, error rates, and resource loading.

We do not use advertising cookies or cookies that track you across unaffiliated third-party websites for advertising purposes.

6. Managing Your Preferences

6.1 Cookie Consent Banner

When you first visit our site, you will see a cookie consent banner that lets you choose which categories of cookies you accept. You can change your preferences at any time by visiting our cookie preferences page.

6.2 How to Disable Cookies

You can control cookies in the following ways:

  • Browser settings: most browsers allow you to block all cookies, block only third-party cookies, or delete existing cookies. Instructions: aboutcookies.org.
  • Opt-out links: Google Analytics opt-out: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.
  • Mobile settings: on mobile devices, you can control cookies through your device's privacy settings.
Note: If you disable strictly necessary cookies, you may not be able to log in or use essential service features.

6.3 Do Not Track

We respect the Do Not Track (DNT) browser setting. If DNT is enabled in your browser, we will not set analytics or marketing cookies. Strictly necessary cookies for security and authentication may still be set.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our use of cookies or to comply with updated legal requirements. The updated policy will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

For material changes, we will provide notice via email or in-product notification at least 30 days before the change takes effect.
